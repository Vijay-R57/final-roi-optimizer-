Synthetic Chennai Sample Drop Optimization dataset.
100 medicine records + 12,000 synthetic Chennai prescribers + exactly 500,000 synthetic prescription/purchase events.
Event dates: 2023-01-01 to 2025-12-31.
Monthly aggregation is included for forecasting.
All prescribers, NPIs, transactions, dates, quantities, costs and relationships are synthetic.
Medicine names/brand examples are real-world labels for research/demo use and are not real prescribing records.
